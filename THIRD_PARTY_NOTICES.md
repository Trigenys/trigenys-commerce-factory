## GeoNames city suggestions

Location suggestions use a modified extract of [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/), licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The data is provided as is, without warranty of completeness or accuracy.

Snapshot retrieved 2026-11-01. For each currently supported country, we retain up to 30 unique populated-place names (feature class P with nonzero reported population), ordered by reported population then GeoNames ID. Population figures and coordinates are not shipped. This is a deliberately limited suggestion list, not address validation. Names remain from the source; accents are normalized only for matching.

Sources and downloaded ZIP digests:
- `CM`: https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/CM.zip; SHA-256 `952958b9d4cb2cdee7e152cc8379bbe12c531063b69cc0cf39c2823f2cc4ff7c`.
- `CI`: https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/CI.zip; SHA-256 `c0ceda87d0020f53919be8b112c4fa00011bdbe0e9218fc38866ff949faaf0b8`.
- `SN`: https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/SN.zip; SHA-256 `525cbbc98b1d5e1258b9847def2f5d06575492372549265e71cb3ae3afbe1faf`.
- `GA`: https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/GA.zip; SHA-256 `eb6551bc239e615b1319a6b60edde919ed7f6807675aa2bd79646a321664d3df`.
- `CG`: https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/CG.zip; SHA-256 `6a6f7893ab669f1cb0d9c774dd8cc44d1f443977991aa538450889af72ff2142`.

Format and licence: https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/readme.txt

The public field credits GeoNames. Runtime search is entirely local; entered addresses are not sent to GeoNames or another geocoding provider.
